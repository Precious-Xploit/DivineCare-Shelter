Providing Care, Shelter, and a Second Chance.

At DivineCare Shelter, we care for the homeless, vulnerable children, and struggling families by providing safe shelter, food, and life-changing support.

About Us

Making the World a Better Place one Step at a Time

Mission

At Divinecare Shelter, our mission is to provide vulnerable and underprivileged children with a safe shelter, quality care, education, emotional support, and opportunities that empower them to grow into confident, responsible, and purpose-driven individuals.

Vision

Our vision is to build a world where every child has access to love, protection, education, and equal opportunities regardless of their background. Divinecare Shelter envisions a future where children are empowered to thrive, break cycles of poverty and neglect, and become positive changemakers in their communities and society at large.

What We Do

Providing Hope Today, Creating Better Tomorrows

Our initiatives are designed to provide urgent support while creating long-term transformation. We respond to critical needs such as care, education, and healthcare, while empowering widows and orphans with practical skills, essential resources, and continuous support to help them achieve stability, independence, and a brighter future.

Shelter & Housing

Providing safe and secure accommodation for the homeless and vulnerable.

Food Support

Daily meals and nutrition programs for those in need.

Education & Empowerment

Skill acquisition and education support for children and adults.

Healthcare Assistance

Basic medical care and wellness support.
