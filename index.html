Providing Care, Shelter, and a Second Chance.

At DivineCare Shelter, we care for the homeless, vulnerable children, and struggling families by providing safe shelter, food, and life-changing support.

Who We Are

About Us

At DivineCare Shelter, we believe that every human life holds value, dignity, and the right to a safe place to call home. Founded on compassion and driven by purpose, our organization exists to support individuals and families facing homelessness, poverty, and hardship.
We understand that behind every struggle is a story—of loss, resilience, and hope waiting to be restored. That is why we go beyond temporary relief.

Providing Shelter

Shelter is more than just a roof over one’s head—it is safety, stability, and the foundation for rebuilding a life. At DivineCare Shelter, we provide secure and welcoming spaces for individuals and families who have been displaced, abandoned, or affected by difficult circumstances.Our shelters are designed to offer more than temporary accommodation. They are environments where people can feel protected, respected, and cared for. We ensure access to clean sleeping areas, basic necessities, and a supportive atmosphere that promotes healing and recovery.

Hospitality

At DivineCare Shelter, hospitality is at the heart of everything we do. We believe that true care goes beyond providing services—it is about creating an environment where every individual feels seen, valued, and welcomed. From the moment someone walks through our doors, they are met with warmth, respect, and compassion. Our team and volunteers are dedicated to building meaningful human connections, ensuring that everyone feels a sense of belonging and acceptance.
